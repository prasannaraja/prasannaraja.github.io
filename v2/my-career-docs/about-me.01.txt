5. ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY & ARCHITECTURAL APPROACH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Prasanna approaches system engineering with an emphasis on maintainability, modularity, testability, and business value alignment:

1. Clean Architecture: Strict separation between domain business rules and external infrastructure (databases, frameworks, UI), ensuring software remains testable, decoupled, and easy to evolve.
2. CQRS (Command Query Responsibility Segregation): Separating mutation (write) operations from query (read) paths, optimizing complex transactional logic on the command side while maximizing speed for analytics and reporting on the query side.
3. Microservices: Decomposing large domains into independently deployable, loosely coupled services aligned around business subdomains and bounded contexts.
4. API-First Design: Defining clear, versioned API contracts before writing implementation code, enabling frontend teams, backend engineers, and third-party consumers to build in parallel.
5. Event-Driven Systems: Utilizing asynchronous events to decouple microservices and handle long-running workflows without tight temporal coupling.
6. Cloud-Native Architecture: Leveraging managed cloud infrastructure (Microsoft Azure, Redis, Serverless, Containerized workloads) for scalability, high availability, and operational simplicity.
7. Data & Analytics Pipelines: Ingesting, transforming, and modeling large datasets through ETL pipelines into high-performance query layers (such as Cube.js) that feed real-time business decision dashboards.
8. AI-Enabled Enterprise Architecture: Integrating Large Language Models (LLMs), RAG pipelines, and Agentic tool-calling workflows as first-class architectural components rather than isolated prototypes.
