About

I’m a senior software engineer with a strong background in enterprise application development, system architecture, and scalable backend platforms — built primarily on .NET and cloud infrastructure, with React and TypeScript on the front end.

Over the past few years, that foundation has extended into data and analytics platforms, including geospatial systems that combine large external datasets with customer and market data to support business decisions. That work is what led me into applied machine learning — and, more recently, into modern AI engineering: LLM integration, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), the Model Context Protocol (MCP), tool calling, agentic workflows, and local LLM infrastructure, largely through Azure AI Foundry.

I see AI as the next layer of enterprise architecture, not a departure from it — my focus is on building production-oriented, AI-enabled applications on top of the same engineering discipline that scalable enterprise systems require.

Approach

A short look at how I think about systems, and where my engineering is heading.

How I Build

Clean Architecture

Separating domain logic from infrastructure so systems stay testable and changeable.

CQRS

Splitting reads from writes to keep complex business logic and reporting paths clean.

Microservices

Decomposing platforms into independently deployable services aligned to business capability.

API-First Design

Designing contracts first so frontend, partners, and integrations can move in parallel.

Event-Driven Systems

Decoupling services with events where workflows are asynchronous by nature.

Cloud-Native Services

Building on managed cloud infrastructure for resilience, scale, and operational simplicity.

Data & Analytics Pipelines

Moving external and operational data through ETL into models people can act on.

AI-Enabled Architecture

Extending platforms with LLMs and agentic workflows as another engineered layer, not a bolt-on.
